### Added
- [Windows, macOS, Linux] Withdraw a cryptocurrency purchase within 14 days from the Account
  screen: the app keeps the purchases it redeemed, sends a declaration signed by your wallet, and
  shows its reference, date and refund amount.
- [Windows, macOS, Linux] Show the renewal notice 30 days before each renewal, with the amount and
  the best tariff, and keep it as a dated PDF in the Account screen. A price increase is announced
  a month ahead and is never charged without that notice.

### Changed
- Always connect with anonymous tokens: the exit server never learns which account is connected.
  A device that holds no token for the current period waits for new tokens instead of connecting
  with the wallet signature.
- [iOS] Deleting the account first unlinks the forum pseudonym from the wallet.
- Update the QUIC library to its latest release, with its security fixes.
- Remove the words "anonymous" and "untraceable" from the app where they overstated the service.
