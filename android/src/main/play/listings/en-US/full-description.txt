<b>ALWAYS-ON OBFUSCATION</b>
Warren tunnels are disguised as ordinary browser traffic (HTTP/3 mimicry over UDP 443). This makes Warren hard to detect or block on restrictive networks, and it cannot be turned off, so every Warren user looks the same on the wire.

<b>HOW DOES WARREN VPN WORK?</b>
With Warren VPN, your traffic travels through an encrypted tunnel to one of our servers and then onward to the website you are visiting. Websites see our server's address instead of yours. Your internet service provider sees only that you are connected to Warren, not what you do online.
It also means the third-party trackers embedded in the websites you visit cannot read your real IP address and use it to follow you from one site to another.

<b>TELEMETRY AND CRASH REPORTS</b>
The app collects a very minimal amount of telemetry and never ties it to your wallet, IP address, or other identifiable information. App logs are never sent automatically: you send them explicitly if you choose to report a problem. App version checks run periodically to tell the app whether an upgrade is available and whether the running version is still supported.

If the split tunneling feature is used, the app queries your system for the list of installed applications. This list is only read inside the split tunneling view and is never sent from the device.
