Warren VPN routes your traffic through an encrypted tunnel to a Warren server, then on to the site you visit. Sites see the server's address instead of yours. Your internet provider sees that you use Warren, not what you do online.

NO ACCOUNT, NO EMAIL
• There is no account to create: no email, no phone number, no name.
• The app creates a wallet for you, protected by a 12-word recovery phrase that only you hold. It is your only credential: write it down and keep it offline.
• Your access is prepaid time attached to that wallet. 7 EUR per month, up to 5 devices at once.

NO ACTIVITY LOGS
• Warren keeps no activity logs: we never record the sites you visit or your DNS queries.
• Our servers run from memory only (RAM-only), rebuilt from a read-only image.
• Warren is operated by WarrenBrowse SRL, a company established in Romania.

LOOKS LIKE ORDINARY WEB TRAFFIC
Warren tunnels run over QUIC and look like ordinary HTTP/3 browsing on port 443. That keeps the connection fast and steady on mobile networks, and hard to tell apart from a regular website visit. This mode is always on, so every Warren user looks the same on the network.

FEATURES
• Kill switch and Always-on VPN support: traffic stays blocked if the tunnel drops.
• Split tunneling: choose apps that bypass the VPN.
• Port forwarding included.
• DAITA, which pads and shapes traffic against traffic analysis.
• Optional DNS content blocking (ads, trackers, malware).
• Servers in several countries.

OPEN SOURCE
The app is open source (GPL-3.0) and its tunnel engine is open source (AGPL-3.0). You can read the code that runs on your phone.

HOW TO START
1. Install the app and create your wallet.
2. Write down your recovery phrase.
3. Add time in the app or on warren.ro, or redeem a voucher.

PRIVACY
The app collects a very minimal amount of technical telemetry and never ties it to your wallet, your IP address or anything that identifies you. App logs leave your device only if you send them yourself to report a problem. The app checks for updates periodically. If you use split tunneling, the app reads the list of installed apps on the device only to show it to you; that list never leaves the device.
